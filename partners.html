For repair shops

Jobs you want, in towns you cover

We collect boat service and parts requests across Cape Cod, the Islands and the South Shore. Approved shops get an anonymous summary and choose which jobs to take.

How leads are delivered

1

A request comes in

The boater gives the boat, engine, job, location, urgency and photos, and agrees to our matching terms.

2

You get a blind offer

We send a private link (copied to you or emailed) with an anonymous summary: boat, engine, service or part, general area, urgency and photos. No name, phone, email or exact marina.

3

Interested or Pass

One tap either way. Add a note and an optional quote range. Links expire after 14 days.

4

We release the contact

When we match the job to you, the same link shows the customer's full details, to your shop only. Everyone else's offer is withdrawn.

Why shops like it

  • No shared leads. A customer's contact goes to one shop, never several.
  • Only your work. Tell us your services, engine brands and towns, and we only offer matching jobs.
  • Fill the slow weeks. Accept more in shoulder season, pass in July. It's up to you.
  • Better-qualified requests. Engine make, model and year, photos and urgency come in up front.
  • Your customer, your price. You quote, schedule and bill directly.

Ways to work together

  • Pay per lead: a fee only for jobs released to you.
  • Exclusive territory: first offer, or the only offer, for your service types in your towns.
  • Own the platform: the site, the domain and the lead system are available to buy or license for a single company.

Terms are agreed in writing before any leads are sent. Ask us for details.
