For repair shops

Get matched with boaters who need you

We collect service requests from boaters across Cape Cod, the Islands and the South Shore, and pass each one to a partner shop that does that work in that area.

How partnering works

  • You choose your work. Tell us your services and the towns or harbors you cover. We only send matching requests.
  • Requests come with details. Boat, engine, location, urgency, notes and photos when the boater adds them.
  • You quote and do the work directly. Your prices, your schedule, your customer relationship.
  • Boaters agree first. Every request includes the boater's consent to share their details with partner shops.

Referral terms are agreed with each shop before any leads are sent. Applying is free and doesn't commit you to anything.

Applying doesn't make you a listed or endorsed shop. We'll contact you to talk it through.
