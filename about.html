Why us

Boat service that runs itself for you

We're a Falmouth-based service-matching company. We don't fix boats. We get your request to the local shop that should, quickly and privately.

Your number stays private

No lead-blasting. Shops see an anonymous summary first, and only the one shop you're matched with gets your contact details.

Matched to your engine

We route by engine brand, type of work, your town and your timing, so the shop that calls you actually works on your boat.

Local, Cape to Islands

From Bourne to Provincetown, Martha's Vineyard and Nantucket, we know the harbors, the seasons and the logistics.

One request, not ten calls

Stop calling around the harbor in May. Send one request and let the right shop come to you.

Dockside and driveway too

Many partner shops are mobile, so a lot of jobs can be done where your boat already is.

Free for boaters

No fees and no obligation. Shops quote you directly, and you only pay for work you approve.

How it works

1

Tell us about the job

Your boat, the engine, what's wrong, where it's kept and how soon you need it. Add photos if you have them. It takes two minutes.

2

We match a shop, privately

Approved local shops that do your kind of work see an anonymous summary: boat, engine, job, town and timing. Shops that can take it tell us.

3

One shop contacts you

We pass your contact details to the one shop that fits best. It confirms details, timing and price, and you decide. You pay the shop directly.

What we are, and what we aren't

  • We are a matching service. We review every request and route it to approved independent shops that do that kind of work in that area.
  • We aren't a repair shop. We don't employ mechanics, set prices or guarantee anyone's work. Ask any shop about its experience, insurance and warranty before you hire it.
  • Shops are vetted before they get requests. They apply, we talk with them, and referral terms are agreed in writing first.
  • Your privacy comes first. Shops see an anonymous summary. Your name and number go to one matched shop, after you're matched.
  • Free for boaters. Partner shops may pay us a referral fee. That never adds a charge from us to your bill.
