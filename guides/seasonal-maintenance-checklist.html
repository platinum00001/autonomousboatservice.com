Seasonal boat maintenance checklist for Cape Cod

Cape boating runs on a calendar. Yards book up in waves, so the trick is to book each job one season ahead. Print this, or keep it on your phone.

January to February

  • Book spring commissioning and any winter projects
  • Check on stored boats after storms (wrap, supports, snow load)
  • Order parts and electronics upgrades early
  • Renew towing and insurance policies and check your cruising area

March to April

  • Renew Massachusetts registration and your town mooring or slip permits
  • Arrange any mooring inspection required by your harbor
  • Bottom paint, zincs and running gear before launch
  • Impeller, filters and fluids per your engine maker's schedule

May to June

  • Commission, launch and do a shakedown run
  • Test bilge pumps, lights, horn, VHF and DSC
  • Check safety gear dates (flares, extinguisher)
  • Note any problems now, while shops have room in their schedules

July to August

  • Clean the bottom mid-season if growth is fast (warm bays)
  • Wash down weekly and check the canvas
  • Book fall haul-out, winterizing and storage
  • Make your hurricane plan: where the boat goes, and who hauls it

September to October

  • Storm watch: know your yard's haul-out list
  • Schedule haul-out and winterizing before the rush
  • Plan winter projects (gelcoat, electronics, repower)
  • Change gear oil and check it for water

November to December

  • Winterize, shrink-wrap and store
  • Batteries charged and on maintainers
  • Get quotes for winter work while the boat is out
  • Bring home electronics and valuables
