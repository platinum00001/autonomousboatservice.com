Boat repair & service in Osterville & Cotuit

One request matches you with an independent local shop for boats in West Bay, North Bay, Cotuit Bay, Prince Cove and the Seapuit River.

Get a quote in Osterville

Osterville and Cotuit share a protected network of bays: West Bay, North Bay and Cotuit Bay. The Seapuit River runs behind Dead Neck, and Prince Cove reaches into Marstons Mills. It's sheltered, beautiful water with a long boatbuilding history.

The fleet here includes classic and wooden boats, larger yachts and many outboard runabouts. Varnish, brightwork, gelcoat and careful indoor winter storage are popular requests, along with the usual engine work.

The bays are shallow in places, and channels shift over time. Check your prop and skeg after the first runs of the season.

We're a service-matching company. Shops in and around Osterville & Cotuit apply to receive requests, and the shop you're matched with does the work and quotes you directly.
