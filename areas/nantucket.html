The Islands

Boat repair & service in Nantucket

One request matches you with an independent local shop for boats in Nantucket Harbor, the Head of the Harbor, Polpis Harbor and Madaket Harbor.

Get a quote in Nantucket

Nantucket is 30 miles out to sea, and that shapes everything about boat service. Island shops are booked well ahead in summer. Parts come over by ferry or air, and the weather decides when they arrive.

Most island boats are outboard-powered runabouts and center consoles on harbor moorings, so mooring service, engine service and fall layup are the common requests. Send full engine details and photos up front, so a shop can line up parts before the visit.

Moorings on Nantucket are managed through the town's harbor rules. Check that your permit and inspections are current before commissioning.

We're a service-matching company. Shops in and around Nantucket apply to receive requests, and the shop you're matched with does the work and quotes you directly.

FAQ

Nantucket questions

Should I book island work earlier than on the Cape?

Yes. Request commissioning in winter and winterizing by midsummer if you can.

Can my boat be serviced on the Cape instead?

Yes, if you can bring it over or arrange transport. Say so in your request and we'll match you on the Cape side.
