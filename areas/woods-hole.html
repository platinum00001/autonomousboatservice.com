Boat repair & service in Woods Hole

One request matches you with an independent local shop for boats in Great Harbor, Eel Pond, Little Harbor and the Woods Hole Passage.

Get a quote in Woods Hole

Woods Hole is a working harbor. There's the ferry terminal, research ships and fishing boats, plus recreational boats tucked into Great Harbor and Eel Pond behind the Water Street drawbridge.

The Woods Hole Passage runs strong currents between Buzzards Bay and Vineyard Sound, so engines and steering need to be dependable here. When an engine is slow to start or has a cooling problem, it's worth fixing before you take the boat through the Hole.

Parking and dock access in the village are tight, especially in summer. When you send a request, say exactly where the boat is and how a mechanic can reach it. Shops can then plan the visit, which is often early in the morning.

We're a service-matching company. Shops in and around Woods Hole apply to receive requests, and the shop you're matched with does the work and quotes you directly.
