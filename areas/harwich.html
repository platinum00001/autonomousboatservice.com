Boat repair & service in Harwich

One request matches you with an independent local shop for boats in Saquatucket Harbor, Wychmere Harbor, Allen Harbor, the Herring River and, on Pleasant Bay, Round Cove.

Get a quote in Harwich

Harwich has three small harbors close together on Nantucket Sound: Saquatucket (the town marina), Wychmere and Allen Harbor. It also has frontage on Pleasant Bay at Round Cove and Pleasant Bay's western shore.

From Harwich Port it's a straight run to Monomoy, Nantucket and the fishing grounds, so offshore-minded center consoles and sportfishing boats make up much of the fleet. Electronics installs and engine service come up a lot.

Slips and moorings in Harwich's harbors are in high demand, so time on the water counts. Booking commissioning early helps you make the most of the season.

We're a service-matching company. Shops in and around Harwich apply to receive requests, and the shop you're matched with does the work and quotes you directly.
