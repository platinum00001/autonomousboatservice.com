Terms of use

Last updated October 19, 2026

We're a service-matching company

Autonomous Boat Service connects boat owners with independent marine repair, service and parts businesses ("partner shops"). We don't perform repairs, sell parts, employ mechanics or supervise any work. Partner shops are independent businesses, not our employees or agents.

No guarantee

  • We don't guarantee that we can match every request, that a shop or part will be available, or how soon a shop will respond.
  • We don't guarantee, warrant or endorse any shop's work, parts, prices, licensing, insurance or timing. Check those directly with the shop before you hire it.
  • Any quote, agreement, payment, warranty, return or dispute is between you and the shop.

Fees

Sending a request is free for boaters. Partner shops may pay us a referral fee. We don't add charges to any shop's bill.

Maintenance plans

Plans are arranged with, priced by and carried out by a partner shop. We help set them up and coordinate them.

Emergencies

This website isn't an emergency service. If anyone is in danger on the water, call 911 or the U.S. Coast Guard on VHF channel 16. For on-water towing, call a towing service directly. Submitting a form doesn't mean help is on the way.

Your information and accounts

Give accurate information and only submit requests for boats you own or are authorized to arrange service for. Accounts are optional and personal to you. By submitting a request, you agree to the two-step sharing described in our privacy policy.

Liability

To the fullest extent allowed by law, Autonomous Boat Service isn't liable for any loss, damage or injury arising from work or parts supplied by a partner shop, or from delays or failures to match a request. The site is provided "as is".

Partner shops

Applying to receive requests creates no obligation for either side. Referral terms are agreed separately in writing before any requests are sent. Offer links are confidential to the shop they were sent to.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts.
