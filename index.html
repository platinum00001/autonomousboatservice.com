How it works

Smart matching, real local techs

"Autonomous" means boat service that runs itself for you. Your request is routed to the right shop, so you don't have to call around the harbor.

Tell us about the job

Boat make, model and length, the engine, what's wrong, where the boat is and how soon you need it. Add photos if you have them.

We match a shop

We look at the service you need, your town or marina, and your timing, then pass your request to a partner shop that fits.

The shop quotes you

The shop contacts you directly to confirm details, timing and price. You decide whether to go ahead and pay the shop directly.

FAQ

Questions boaters ask

Are you a boat repair shop?

No. Autonomous Boat Service is a referral service. We take your request and connect you with independent local marine repair shops that do that kind of work in your area. The shop you choose does the work and gives you its own quote.

What does it cost?

Sending a request is free and there's no obligation. You pay the shop directly for any work you approve. Partner shops may pay us a referral fee; that never adds anything to your bill from us.

Why "autonomous"?

It's about boat service that runs itself for you: one short form, and your request gets routed to a shop that fits your boat, your location and your timing. Robots don't work on your boat. Real, local marine techs do.

How fast will I hear back?

We review requests as they come in and try to match you quickly. Timing depends on the season, the job and which shops have openings. Spring launch and fall haul-out are the busiest times, so earlier is better.

What if it's an emergency on the water?

If anyone is in danger, call 911 or hail the Coast Guard on VHF channel 16 right away. If you have a towing membership, call it directly. For repairs after you're safe, choose "Emergency" on the form and we'll prioritize it.

Who are the partner shops?

Independent marine businesses on Cape Cod, the Islands and the South Shore that apply to receive requests. We don't employ the techs or guarantee their work, so ask any shop about its experience, insurance and warranty before you hire it.

Can someone come to my slip or mooring?

Often, yes. Choose "Mobile / dockside service" or mention it in your request. Many local shops do dockside and driveway work.

What happens to my information?

We share your request only with the partner shop or shops we match you with, and only because you agreed to it on the form. We never sell your information. You can ask us to delete it at any time.
