How it works

Smart matching, real local mechanics

"Autonomous" means boat service that runs itself for you. Your request is routed to the right shop, so you don't have to call around the harbor.

1

Tell us about the job

Your boat, the engine, what's wrong, where it's kept and how soon you need it. Add photos if you have them. It takes two minutes.

2

We match a shop, privately

Approved local shops that do your kind of work see an anonymous summary: boat, engine, job, town and timing. Shops that can take it tell us.

3

One shop contacts you

We pass your contact details to the one shop that fits best. It confirms details, timing and price, and you decide. You pay the shop directly.

FAQ

Questions boaters ask

Are you a boat repair shop?

No. Autonomous Boat Service is a service-matching company. We take your request and connect you with an independent local marine shop that does that kind of work in your area. The shop does the work and gives you its own quote.

What does it cost?

Sending a request is free, with no obligation. You pay the shop directly for any work you approve. Partner shops may pay us a referral fee, and that never adds anything to your bill from us.

Who sees my phone number?

Only us, and then one shop. When we look for a match, shops first see an anonymous summary: boat, engine, the job, your general area and timing. Your name and contact details go to one matched shop, only after you're matched.

Why "autonomous"?

Because the service runs itself for you. You fill in one short form, and your request is routed to a shop that fits your boat, location and timing. Robots don't work on your boat. Real local marine mechanics do.

How fast will I hear back?

We review requests as they come in and try to match you quickly. Timing depends on the season, the job and which shops have openings. Spring launch and fall haul-out are the busiest times, so earlier is better.

What if it's an emergency on the water?

If anyone is in danger, call 911 or hail the Coast Guard on VHF channel 16 right away. If you have a towing membership, call it directly. For the repair, once you're safe, choose "Emergency" on the form and we'll prioritize it.

Who are the partner shops?

Independent marine businesses on Cape Cod, the Islands and the South Shore that apply and are approved to receive requests. We don't employ the mechanics or guarantee their work, so ask any shop about its experience, insurance and warranty before you hire it.

Can I find a part through you?

Yes. Use the parts request form with the part name or number and your engine or boat details. We pass it to partner shops and parts counters. We don't stock parts or set prices.

Run a marine repair shop?

Get anonymous job requests in your towns. Say yes only to the ones you want.
