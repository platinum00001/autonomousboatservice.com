Privacy policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Autonomous Boat Service ("we") runs this website to connect boaters with independent marine repair shops. This policy explains what we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Quote requests: your name, phone, optional email, boat make, model and length, engine, location or marina, the service you need, how urgent it is, your notes and any photos you upload.
  • Partner shop applications: shop name, contact name, phone, email, website, services and service area.
  • Basic technical data: to stop spam, we keep a one-way, salted fingerprint of your IP address that changes daily. We don't use advertising trackers or sell data.
  • Staff logins: staff sign in with an email address. There are no public accounts.

How we use and share it

  • We use your request to find a suitable repair shop, and we share it with the partner shop or shops we match you with. You agree to this when you tick the consent box. Those shops may contact you about your job.
  • We don't sell your information or share it for marketing by others.
  • Our service providers store data for us: Supabase (database and photo storage) and GitHub Pages (website hosting).

How long we keep it

We keep requests for as long as needed to arrange the work and handle follow-up, generally up to 24 months, then delete them. You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

Delete my data

To have your request and photos deleted, send the phone number or email you used below. We'll delete what we hold and confirm using that contact. Shops you were already matched with keep their own records under their own policies, and we'll pass your request on to them when we can.
