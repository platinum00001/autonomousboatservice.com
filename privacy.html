Privacy policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

Autonomous Boat Service ("we") runs this website to connect boaters with independent marine repair shops and parts suppliers ("partner shops"). This policy explains what we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Service and parts requests: your name, phone, optional email, boat and engine details, location or marina, the service or part you need, how urgent it is, your notes and any photos you upload.
  • Optional customer accounts: if you sign in, we store your email address so we can show you your own requests.
  • Partner shop applications: shop name, contact name, phone, email, website, services and service area.
  • Basic technical data: to stop spam, we keep a one-way, salted fingerprint of your IP address that changes daily. We don't use advertising trackers or sell data.

How we share it: two steps

  1. Anonymous summary. To find a shop, we send approved partner shops a summary of your request: boat make, model, length and engine, the service or part, the town or general area, urgency and your photos. Any phone numbers or emails in your notes are removed. Your name, phone, email and exact marina are not included.
  2. One matched shop. Only after you're matched with a shop do we share your contact details, with that one shop only, so it can contact you about the job.

You agree to this when you tick the consent box. We don't sell your information or share it for marketing by others. Our service providers store data for us: Supabase (database, sign-in and photo storage) and GitHub Pages (website hosting).

How long we keep it

We keep requests for as long as needed to arrange the work and handle follow-up, generally up to 24 months, then delete them. Shop offer links expire after 14 days. You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

Delete my data

To have your request and photos deleted, send the phone number or email you used below. We'll delete what we hold and confirm using that contact. A shop you were already matched with keeps its own records under its own policy, and we'll pass your request on to it when we can.
