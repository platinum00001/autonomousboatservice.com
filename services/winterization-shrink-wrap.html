Boat winterization & shrink-wrap

On the Cape, fall layup fills up fast. Most boats come out of the water between late September and November, and the good time slots go early. Send one request and we'll match you with a shop that can winterize the boat where it sits and wrap it for the winter.

Get a quote Call

What's included

  • Fuel stabilizer and topping off, or draining, per the engine maker's advice
  • Engine flushing and freeze protection for outboards, inboards and sterndrives
  • Lower-unit gear oil change (it shows water intrusion before it freezes)
  • Fresh-water, head and livewell systems with non-toxic antifreeze
  • Battery removal or a maintenance-charging plan
  • Shrink-wrap with vents and supports, or fitted covers
  • Coordination of the haul-out and blocking at a yard, or trailer storage at home

Signs you need it

  • Your boat stays in the water past mid-October
  • You don't know when the engine last had its gear oil changed
  • Last spring you found a cracked fitting or a dead battery
  • Your marina needs boats wrapped or moved by a set date

On Cape Cod & the Islands

Cape Cod winters swing above and below freezing over and over, and that's what cracks blocks, manifolds and plumbing. Water left anywhere it can freeze is the main risk. Wind off Buzzards Bay and Cape Cod Bay is the other: shrink-wrap should be tight, well supported and vented so it doesn't trap moisture and mildew.

Questions to ask any shop

  • Is winterizing priced flat by engine type, or by the hour?
  • Do you add fuel stabilizer, fog the engine or drain the carburetors, and which does my engine maker recommend?
  • Is shrink-wrap vented, and does it include a door for access?
  • Can you also do the spring commissioning, and do I keep my spot on the list?

FAQ

Winterization & shrink-wrap: common questions

When should I book winterizing on Cape Cod?

As early as you can. Most yards build their fall schedule in August and September. If you plan to keep boating into October, book anyway and ask for a late slot.

Can I winterize at home in my driveway?

Yes. Many mobile shops winterize and shrink-wrap trailered boats in the driveway. Check your town and HOA rules on storing a boat at home.

Is shrink-wrap better than a canvas cover?

Shrink-wrap is tight and keeps snow and water out for one season, then it's recycled or thrown out. A good fitted winter cover costs more up front and lasts for years. Ask the shop what it recommends for your boat and where it's stored.
